## 🚀 Featured Projects

---

### 🧠 EEG-Based Neurological Disease Detection
> **Alzheimer's Disease & Frontotemporal Dementia Classification**

Developed two novel EEG representations — functional brain connectivity graphs and 3D connectivity matrices — and benchmarked **GNNs vs 3D-CNNs** for neurological disease classification. The 3D-CNN approach achieved superior results.

**Tech Stack:** `Python` `PyTorch` `Graph Neural Networks` `3D-CNN` `EEG Signal Processing` `MNE`

📄 *Published at **IEEE ECCE 2025*** · DOI: [10.1109/ECCE64574.2025.11013802](https://doi.org/10.1109/ECCE64574.2025.11013802)

---

### 🤰 Fetal Head Segmentation & Measurement
> **Residual U-Net for Ultrasound Imaging**

Built a **Residual U-Net** architecture to segment fetal heads in ultrasound images and automatically measure head circumference — supporting clinical prenatal care workflows.

**Tech Stack:** `Python` `TensorFlow` `Keras` `U-Net` `Medical Imaging` `OpenCV`

📄 *Published at **IEEE ICICT 2024*** · DOI: [10.1109/ICICT64387.2024.10839692](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICICT64387.2024.10839692)

---

### 🔬 Anatomy-Agnostic Medical Segmentation *(Under Review)*
> **Prompt-Conditioned Channel Attention for Hierarchical Feature Modulation**

A novel prompt-conditioned attention mechanism for hierarchical feature modulation enabling anatomy-agnostic segmentation across diverse medical imaging domains.

**Tech Stack:** `PyTorch` `Transformer Attention` `Medical Segmentation` `Multi-organ`

📄 *Under review at **IEEE Journal of Translational Engineering in Health and Medicine (JTEHM)***

---

### 🤖 Agentic RAG System
> **LLM-Powered Retrieval-Augmented Generation Pipeline**

Designed and deployed production-grade **multi-agent AI workflows** with tool-calling, autonomous reasoning chains, and RAG pipelines. Integrated with ServiceNow and fine-tuned Gemma 3 for efficiency.

**Tech Stack:** `LangGraph` `LangChain` `FastAPI` `FAISS` `ChromaDB` `OpenAI API` `Gemma 3` `Docker`

### 🌐 Agentic Federated Learning *(Ongoing)*
> **Addressing Non-IID Data Drift in Federated Systems**

Developing Agentic AI-driven Federated Learning strategies to mitigate **client drift** and performance degradation from heterogeneous (non-IID) data distributions across decentralized clients.

**Tech Stack:** `PyTorch` `Federated Learning` `LangGraph` `Agentic AI` `Apache Airflow`
